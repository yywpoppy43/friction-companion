## The triad structure (governs the whole matrix)

Nine types, three centers, by how each relates to a core emotion:

- **Body / Gut center (8, 9, 1) — core emotion: anger.** 8 externalizes anger; 1 internalizes it as resentment; 9 numbs/falls asleep to it. Concern: control, autonomy, resistance. Get "stuck in the body."
- **Heart / Image center (2, 3, 4) — core emotion: shame.** 2 externalizes shame (focus on others); 3 loses contact with it (merges with a role); 4 internalizes it as deficiency. Concern: identity, image, worth in others' eyes.
- **Head center (5, 6, 7) — core emotion: fear.** 5 internalizes fear (withdraws to gather resources); 6 splits on it (anxiety/doubt); 7 externalizes/outruns it (into options and plans). Concern: security, anxiety-management, mental orientation.

This is load-bearing: the center tells you *which* raw emotion the defense is managing, and that must stay consistent per type through every layer.

## The Hornevian groups — social style [STRUCTURE]

*Karen Horney's three social strategies, applied to the Enneagram by Riso & Hudson. Where the Center says what a type **wants**, the Hornevian group says **how it goes and gets it**.*

- **Assertive — moving *against* (3, 7, 8).** Insist or demand. Under friction: mobilize, drive into it.
- **Withdrawn — moving *away* (4, 5, 9).** Move away from engagement. Under friction: pull back, go quiet.
- **Compliant / Dutiful — moving *toward* (1, 2, 6).** Do what is expected or required. Under friction: track, manage, keep the surface right.

**The honest caveats, both real:**
- This is the **Riso-Hudson** assignment specifically. **Naranjo sorts differently** (Against 1-3-8, Toward 7-9-2, Away 5-6-4), and at least one source argues Naranjo's is *more faithful to Horney's original*. We use Riso-Hudson; we say which we use, and we never call the alignment "independent" — if you used Naranjo's, it wouldn't align.
- The Hornevian groups are an Enneagram sub-theory. [STRUCTURE], never [ESTABLISHED].

**The 3×3 that falls out.** Each Hornevian group contains exactly one type from each Center:

| | Body (anger) | Heart (shame) | Head (fear) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assertive** *(against)* | **8** | **3** | **7** |
| **Withdrawn** *(away)* | **9** | **4** | **5** |
| **Compliant** *(toward)* | **1** | **2** | **6** |

Center × Hornevian fills all nine cells, no duplicates. **Use this as a coherence check, never as a generator** — it predicts the *shape* of a type's signature, not its *specifics*. You cannot derive "the masseter clenches" from "Compliant × Body." Compressing the nine into 3×3 for authoring would cost exactly the nuance that makes a type recognizable in a room.

It also disambiguates the thing that reads wrong: **Type 1 in a "Compliant/affiliative" mode looks incoherent** until you add the Center. Type 1 moves toward *the standard*; Type 2 moves toward *people*. Body-center compliance and Heart-center compliance are not the same animal.

## The nine types — canonical fields

Format per type: **Name (Riso-Hudson) · classical/alternate name · Center · Core Fear · Core Desire · Passion (vice) · Virtue · Fixation · Holy Idea · Classical ego defense.** All fields sourced.

### Type 1 — The Reformer (a.k.a. The Perfectionist)
- **Center:** Body / anger (internalized as resentment)
- **Core Fear:** being corrupt, defective, bad, wrong
- **Core Desire:** to have integrity, to be good, right, balanced
- **Passion:** Anger (as chronic, suppressed resentment at the gap between what is and what should be)
- **Virtue:** Serenity
- **Fixation:** Resentment
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Perfection
- **Ego defense:** **Reaction formation** (expressing the opposite of a forbidden impulse — e.g. masking anger with extreme politeness/correctness)

### Type 2 — The Helper (a.k.a. The Giver)
- **Center:** Heart / shame (externalized — focus on others' needs)
- **Core Fear:** being unwanted, unworthy of love, unneeded
- **Core Desire:** to feel loved, wanted
- **Passion:** Pride ("I am the one who can help you; I don't have needs")
- **Virtue:** Humility
- **Fixation:** Flattery / Ingratiation
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Freedom / Holy Will
- **Ego defense:** **Repression** (of one's own needs and "unacceptable" feelings, replaced with an acceptable helpful emotion)

### Type 3 — The Achiever (a.k.a. The Performer)
- **Center:** Heart / shame (out of contact — merges with a successful image)
- **Core Fear:** being worthless, a failure, without value apart from achievement
- **Core Desire:** to feel valuable, successful, worthwhile
- **Passion:** Deceit (in the Enneagram sense: self-deception — becoming the image, losing the authentic self; not primarily lying to others)
- **Virtue:** Truthfulness / Authenticity
- **Fixation:** Vanity
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Hope / Holy Law
- **Ego defense:** **Identification** (fusing identity with a role/image that earns approval)

### Type 4 — The Individualist (a.k.a. The Romantic)
- **Center:** Heart / shame (internalized as a sense of deficiency)
- **Core Fear:** having no identity or personal significance; being defective, missing something others have
- **Core Desire:** to be uniquely themselves, to find identity and significance
- **Passion:** Envy (comparing, longing for what is missing/what others have)
- **Virtue:** Equanimity
- **Fixation:** Melancholy
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Origin
- **Ego defense:** **Introjection** (taking in criticism/the negative and turning it inward; also artistic **sublimation** per Naranjo)

### Type 5 — The Investigator (a.k.a. The Observer)
- **Center:** Head / fear (internalized — withdraw and conserve)
- **Core Fear:** being helpless, incapable, depleted, overwhelmed; invaded/engulfed
- **Core Desire:** to be capable and competent; to understand; self-sufficiency
- **Passion:** Avarice (of self, energy, resources, contact — hoarding to not be depleted)
- **Virtue:** Non-attachment / Generosity
- **Fixation:** Stinginess / Retention
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Omniscience / Holy Transparency
- **Ego defense:** **Isolation** (isolating thought from feeling; compartmentalizing to avoid being flooded)

### Type 6 — The Loyalist (a.k.a. The Skeptic / Loyal Skeptic)
- **Center:** Head / fear (splits on it — anxiety and doubt; phobic and counter-phobic)
- **Core Fear:** being without support or guidance; unable to survive on one's own; danger
- **Core Desire:** to have security, support, certainty, guidance
- **Passion:** Fear (as anxiety — scanning for threat, doubting)
- **Virtue:** Courage
- **Fixation:** Cowardice / Doubt
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Faith / Holy Strength
- **Ego defense:** **Projection** (attributing inner doubt/threat onto the outer world and testing it)

### Type 7 — The Enthusiast (a.k.a. The Epicure)
- **Center:** Head / fear (externalized/outrun — flee pain into options)
- **Core Fear:** being deprived, trapped in pain, limited; missing out
- **Core Desire:** to be satisfied, content; to have needs met and keep options open
- **Passion:** Gluttony (for experience, stimulation, options — never enough)
- **Virtue:** Sobriety
- **Fixation:** Planning / Anticipation
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Wisdom / Holy Work / Holy Plan
- **Ego defense:** **Rationalization** (reframing pain/limits into positives; explaining away)

### Type 8 — The Challenger (a.k.a. The Protector)
- **Center:** Body / anger (externalized — full force)
- **Core Fear:** being controlled, harmed, violated, made vulnerable/powerless
- **Core Desire:** to protect themselves; to be in control of their own life and destiny; self-reliance
- **Passion:** Lust (in the Enneagram sense: excess, intensity, big energy — not primarily sexual)
- **Virtue:** Innocence
- **Fixation:** Vengeance / Objectification
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Truth
- **Ego defense:** **Denial** (of vulnerability, weakness, and their own softness/need)

### Type 9 — The Peacemaker (a.k.a. The Mediator)
- **Center:** Body / anger (asleep to it — self-forgetting)
- **Core Fear:** loss, fragmentation, separation, conflict; being shut out
- **Core Desire:** inner and outer peace, stability, wholeness, harmony
- **Passion:** Sloth (in the Enneagram sense: self-forgetting, inaction toward one's own priorities — not physical laziness)
- **Virtue:** Right Action / Engagement
- **Fixation:** Indolence / Daydreaming
- **Holy Idea:** Holy Love
- **Ego defense:** **Narcotization** (numbing/dissociating — dulling inner life with routine, comfort, merging)
