Poppy Lifeforce

Since humans arrived, we’ve reached to make sense of the world. Everything that has been discovered was already there before we understood it. The answers were not the bottleneck — the questions were. The universe holds everything and explains none of it, as we grow to know enough to ask the right questions.

A question is only as good as the mind that asks it — so the long project of our species has been extending that mind. We built instruments to see farther, to think faster, to last longer, and we guarded our own survival so humanity would be here to keep looking.

For a long time, the answer has been the universe — reality itself.

The work was to ask the right questions. So we built phenomenal tools, and bought ourselves time to keep asking.

They work.

Here is the other direction.

The answer is you.

What do we ask now?

We’ve poured our evolution into understanding the world out there. What happens if we reverse it — turn all that gathered momentum around — and the answer is you: the one who wants, who asks, who reaches when the pressure comes?

Here, the answer sits the way it has always sat out there — whole, already present, waiting on the right question.
