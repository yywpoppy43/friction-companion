# 1. HARDWARE — the fixed and fluid functions

## 1.1 Core settings

### Type — Generator
- **id:** type
- **what it is:** the work engine (see `region.engine`) is fixed. That makes her a Generator. About 70% of people share this. `[read]`
- **what it gives:** deep, renewable work energy. Can go hard, all day, for years, on something right.
- **the condition:** the engine doesn't start on command. It starts on contact. Something has to arrive and the body has to respond.
- **when it works:** something shows up, a pull in the gut before thinking, work is hard but doesn't cost, ends the day emptied and satisfied.
- **when it doesn't:** she decided something was a good idea, reasoned it out, started. Same hours, but it drains disproportionately. Heavy before beginning. Constant re-motivation. That's the engine refusing a false key.
- **connects to:** region.engine, strategy, authority, signature, not-self
- **source:** HD · the book: type

### Strategy — wait to respond
- **id:** strategy
- **what it is:** not passivity. Stay busy, stay in range of things, let the body decide which ones. The ignition cannot be manufactured.
- **the hard part:** the mind keeps proposing good ideas and the body keeps declining them. That reads as laziness. It isn't.
- **connects to:** type, mechanism.no-incoming
- **source:** HD · the book: type

### Authority — the gut
- **id:** authority
- **what it is:** the decision-maker is an immediate response in the body — a sound or sensation, before language. Yes/no questions work. Open-ended "what should I do" questions don't.
- **why it's trustworthy for her specifically:** she has no emotional wave of her own (see `region.emotion`). People with fixed emotions must wait out a cycle before deciding. She never has to. `[firm]`
- **the specific risk:** she builds by talking, and talking is where the mind argues with the gut and wins because it has more words. Check whether the first non-verbal reaction and the talked-to conclusion match. When they diverge, the first was correct.
- **split-definition note:** take slightly more time than the gut alone suggests. Same response twice, on separate days, is more reliable than once.
- **connects to:** region.engine, region.emotion, split, conflict.decision-seat
- **source:** HD · the book: type; first fitted read

### Signature — satisfaction
- **id:** signature
- **what it is:** the signal it's working. A physical settling at the end of a day. Tired in the right way.
- **source:** HD · first read

### Not-self — frustration
- **id:** not-self
- **what it is:** the off-signal. Not tiredness, not sadness. Frustration specifically.
- **only two causes:** she started something instead of responding to something, or she's still inside something the gut already closed.
- **why it matters more for her:** in a pressure system, frustration is pressure with nowhere to go, and it reads in real time. This is her gauge — not "am I doing well" but "is the pressure moving." `[firm]`
- **connects to:** type, mechanism.pressure-differential
- **source:** HD · the book: type; "is it powerful" turn

### Definition — split
- **id:** split
- **what it is:** the fixed functions form two separate islands that do not touch. Island A: mental pressure + processing (the mind). Island B: identity + will + engine + instinct + pressure (the body). The gap sits at the outlet.
- **what it means:** the mind and the body run as two independent systems on separate clocks. Understanding something does not cause action. Acting does not require understanding first. One does not produce the other, ever. `[read]` — flagged for her to check; she has not confirmed or denied this
- **her answer, September 10, 2026:** when something of hers goes out, the mental circling stops. Not reduces — stops. The stuck feeling and the flatness go with it, immediately. The longer she was isolated before the exit, the stronger that relief. `[hers]` — this partly answers the `[read]` claim above. The two islands still exchange no load: there is no wire, and an exit does not empty the mind. What is not independent is the *rate* — the circling is coupled to whether anything is getting out.
- **how it shows:** understanding exactly what to do and being unable to move (not procrastination — no wire). Doing things and understanding afterward why. A felt sense of being two people.
- **what follows:** don't wait for clarity before moving. Certain people close the gap and produce a feeling of wholeness that fires regardless of whether they're good for her — test by what gets made, not by how it feels.
- **the three possible bridges:** three wires could connect the outlet to the mind. She holds one half of one (see `sealed.precise-naming`). None is complete.
- **connects to:** region.head, region.mind, region.throat, sealed.precise-naming, mechanism.knowing-vs-doing
- **source:** HD · the book: the split

### Profile — 3/6, experimenter / role model
- **id:** profile
- **line 3, conscious — the experimenter:** learns only by trying and breaking. Nothing abstract sticks. Has to be in the hands and go wrong. Knowledge from contact is the reliable kind. Cost: a high rate of things not working; third lines experience decades of this as failure before the reframe lands in the body. Instruction: stop trying to get it right the first time — get it wrong faster. `[firm]` — matches her stated learning style
- **line 6, unconscious — the role model:** three phases. Birth to about 30: lives like a 3rd line, doubled with the conscious 3rd line — very high experiment, very high breakage. About 30 to 50: "on the roof" — steps back, observes rather than participates, less appetite for throwing in; feels like losing momentum and isn't. About 50 onward: comes off the roof, lives what was learned.
- **where she is:** born 1996, turned 30 in 2026. At the first hinge now. A pull to withdraw and observe is the 6th line shifting on schedule. `[read]`
- **the combination:** a life built from experiment that eventually becomes usable by others. Wisdom is a late product.
- **connects to:** convergence.late-payoff, elements.decade
- **source:** HD · the book: profile

### Life theme — Right Angle Cross of Maya
- **id:** life-theme
- **right angle:** personal destiny. Her life is about her own process, not about meeting others' arcs. About 70% of people share this — it's directional, not rare. `[read]`
- **maya:** the world of form and appearance. Full immersion in material experience, learning to tell what lasts from what only looks like it.
- **the four capacities that make it up:**
  - conscious sun — **completion**, seeing cycles all the way through → `sealed.completion` (no wire)
  - conscious earth — **durability instinct**, recognizing staying power before it's proven → wired via `wire.transformation`

- **the unconscious axis:** mystery on one end, exact detail on the other. Her body's job, whether or not the mind agrees: grounding the unnameable into something specific enough to name.
- **the structural catch:** the primary conscious capacity — completion — has no wire. The most emphasized thing in the design has no mechanism. `[firm]`
- **connects to:** sealed.completion, sealed.precise-naming, wire.circling-mind, wire.transformation
- **source:** HD · the book: the cross; first fitted read

### Variable — four arrows

- **what it is:** two arrows point left, two right. Broadly, left = active, focused, strategic; right = passive, receptive, peripheral.
- **hold loosely:** this is the layer with the most elaborate machinery and the least consistent teaching between practitioners. `[uncertain]`

## 1.2 The nine regions

### Mental pressure — FIXED
- **id:** region.head
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** constant, self-generated mental pressure. Questions arrive without anything prompting them. About 70% of people have this open and get their mental pressure from outside. Hers comes from inside and doesn't stop.
- **gives:** never needs external stimulation to get the mind going.
- **costs:** can't be turned off — doesn't respond to tiredness, vacation, or deciding the question doesn't matter. Pressures other people mentally — a question she asks lodges in someone with an open head for days. Generates questions faster than any body can act on; most aren't meant to be acted on.
- **island:** A (mind)
- **wires from here:** wire.circling-mind
- **connects to:** region.mind, split
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature

### Processing — FIXED
- **id:** region.mind
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** a fixed way of thinking. How she breaks something down, what counts as understanding, the shape of analysis — the same at 20 and at 60.
- **gives:** mentally reliable. Can be certain. Can hold a position. A consistent lens.
- **costs:** certainty is structural, not evidential — the feeling of being sure is identical whether right or wrong. Cannot think in a different shape; when the lens doesn't fit, applies it harder. `[firm]` — she has confirmed the mind is "for understanding, not deciding"
- **rule:** the mind is for understanding. The gut is for deciding. Both are strong; they do different jobs.
- **island:** A (mind)
- **wires from here:** wire.circling-mind
- **connects to:** region.head, split, authority, conflict.decision-seat
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature; "most people" turn

### The outlet — FLUID
- **id:** region.throat
- **state:** fluid
- **what it is:** the only place anything in her becomes real in the outside world. Not a metaphor — a rule of the system. No other function can express. The other eight are rooms; this is the door.
- **defined vs open:** sixteen possible wires could run into it. She has zero completed. About 28–30% of people have this open. `[read]`
- **what fluid means here:** access to expression is not consistent. Some days the words are exactly there; other days, same idea, gone. Practice doesn't fix it.
- **her resolution of the apparent conflict with the elemental reading:** the elements say her generation is continuous; this says her expression is inconsistent. Both true — one is *capacity*, the other is *vehicle*. She always has the thoughts. Whether they land in the form she meant, on a given day, varies. `[hers]`
- **conditioning:** pressure to speak in order to be noticed — talking to prove existence, jumping in, filling silence, explaining. The other face: going silent and feeling unheard.
- **what's true:** cannot make herself recognized by pushing. Works through being invited. When asked, the words come. When pushed, they don't.
- **the wisdom that forms:** enormous adaptability of voice — many registers, becoming the right voice for the situation, saying what's needed rather than what's hers. Only works when speech is a response.
- **her native modes (from the two sealed capacities here):** naming things precisely, and letting made work carry the message. Not broadcast. Not persuasion.
- **the correction to an earlier overstatement:** "no motor reaching the outlet" is not rare — it's the definition of a Generator (~37% of people). What is uncommon is the combination: seven of nine fixed *and* the door open. Everything internal consistent; the one outward-facing function variable. `[corrected]`
- **the gap:** the split sits here. This is where the two islands would meet and don't.
- **sealed capacities here:** sealed.precise-naming, sealed.demonstrated-craft
- **the one missing piece:** see `missing-piece.now`
- **island:** neither — the gap
- **connects to:** split, missing-piece.now, sealed.precise-naming, sealed.demonstrated-craft, convergence.no-exit, mechanism.pressure-differential
- **source:** HD · the throat turn; system built in one pass; her capacity/vehicle resolution

### Identity and direction — FIXED
- **id:** region.identity
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** she knows who she is — not philosophically, structurally. The sense of self doesn't move. Same alone at 3am as in a room she's trying to impress. About half of people don't have this. `[read]`
- **also:** fixed direction. A heading that doesn't change. May not be able to articulate it, but always knows when off it — the wrongness registers even when the rightness can't be named.
- **gives:** doesn't try on identities. Can be around very sure people and not be pulled; around lost people and not get lost. Impatient with people who don't know who they are — worth watching, because for them it isn't a choice.
- **costs:** can't be talked out of a direction, including a wrong one — only her own body will move it. May not understand that others need help finding themselves. May mistake fixed identity for correct identity.
- **island:** B (body)
- **wires from here:** wire.initiate, wire.following-convictions, wire.perfected-form
- **connects to:** integration-circuit, wire.initiate
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature

### Will and worth — FIXED
- **id:** region.will
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** consistent willpower. Not motivation — will. The capacity to decide to do something and do it without needing to feel like it. A promise holds after the enthusiasm drains. About 35% of people have this. **The rarest thing she has.** `[firm]` — she confirmed "am I enough" is not a live question
- **worth:** internal. No background process of proving herself. The 65% with this open spend real energy on "am I enough." That question isn't live in her.
- **costs:** overestimates what others can commit to — makes a promise and keeps it, so assumes everyone can; reads their non-follow-through as character failure when it's often capacity. Bargains — wants a deal, something for something; may be unable to just receive or just give. Can overcommit will and burn it. Can force through work the gut said no to — it works for a while, and it's the most expensive thing this function can do.
- **use:** deliberately, for the unglamorous middle of projects that nobody sees. And critically — **this is the equipment that makes the deposit design possible.** A commitment made in advance holds when the enthusiasm is gone. See `design.deposit-structure`.
- **sealed capacity here:** sealed.material-control
- **island:** B (body)
- **wires from here:** wire.initiate
- **connects to:** wire.initiate, sealed.material-control, design.deposit-structure, mechanism.autonomy-resolved
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature

### The work engine — FIXED
- **id:** region.engine
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** the body's engine. Deep, renewable work energy. Also where yes and no live — the decision is the same organ as the energy.
- **starts on contact, not on command.** Something arrives; the body responds or doesn't. That response is the ignition. There is no other.
- **sealed capacities here:** sealed.resource-direction, sealed.completion — note that the two hanging capacities in the engine are *directing resources* and *completing*. Both missing routes. `[firm]`
- **island:** B (body)

- **connects to:** type, authority, integration-circuit, sealed.completion, sealed.resource-direction, mechanism.finishing-broken-down

### Instinct — FIXED
- **id:** region.instinct
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** immediate, non-verbal, in-the-moment knowing. Registers safety, danger, health, and timing before the mind has anything to say.
- **how it speaks:** once. Quietly. Present tense. Does not repeat, does not explain. The fastest system in the body and the easiest to talk over.
- **the three things it reads:** safety (whether a person, place, situation is okay — the first read is almost always accurate). Health (what her body needs now — not what's healthy in the abstract). Timing (whether *now* is the moment — not whether the thing is good).
- **how it shows:** a quiet no about someone everyone likes. A sudden knowing to leave. Physical reluctance she can't justify. Then the loud fixed mind overrides it, something goes wrong, and she says "I knew."
- **costs:** fear lives here structurally — fears that don't leave because they're built in, not situational. The job is distinguishing instinct fear (real, present, act on it) from mental fear (imagined future, ignore). Expects others to be as fast; they aren't.
- **practical instruction:** when the body says something once, quietly, unjustifiably — note it. Don't demand a reason.
- **island:** B (body)
- **wires from here:** wire.perfected-form, wire.power, wire.transformation
- **connects to:** integration-circuit, wire.transformation
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature

### Emotion — FLUID
- **id:** region.emotion
- **state:** fluid
- **what it is:** her emotional state is not consistently hers. Takes in the emotional weather of whoever she's with and amplifies it. Much of what she experiences as her mood is the room's mood at higher volume.
- **unusually loud for an open function:** three sealed capacities sit here (see below). Strong feelings, frequently borrowed.
- **conditioning:** avoiding confrontation to keep the emotional field calm. She feels the disturbance more than the other person does, so she swallows truth to prevent it. **The single most predictable failure pattern in the chart.** `[read]`
- **the wisdom that forms:** reads emotional fields with real accuracy. Knows what's happening in a room before anyone speaks.
- **the payoff that matters:** because there is no wave of her own, the gut is trustworthy in the moment. See `authority`.
- **critical for the pressure reading:** because emotion is the fluid part, pressure with no exit does not announce itself as pressure. It gets read as depression, flatness, futility — as a fact about the situation rather than as a hydraulic state. `[arguable]` — see `mechanism.pressure-differential`
- **sealed capacities here:** sealed.melancholy-swing, sealed.principles, sealed.social-openness
- **island:** neither — open
- **connects to:** authority, mechanism.pressure-differential, mechanism.melancholy-three-sources
- **source:** HD · the book: open centers; pressure differential turn

### Pressure and drive — FIXED
- **id:** region.pressure
- **state:** fixed
- **what it is:** she generates her own pressure. The stress that gets her moving comes from inside, at her own rate, on her own schedule.
- **gives:** largely immune to other people's urgency. Someone panicking nearby doesn't make her panic. Has a natural rhythm of intensity — periods where pressure builds and drives hard, then drops — that syncs with no one's calendar. Unbothered by deadlines that terrify others; driven by things nobody else finds urgent.
- **costs:** pressures other people without noticing — this function radiates; people near her feel driven and rushed and won't know why. May not know when to stop — the pressure is internal, so there's no external signal that it's over. External deadlines mostly read as static.
- **the one exception:** completion. See `sealed.completion` — the only place to deliberately install outside pressure.
- **sealed capacities here:** sealed.begin-experience, sealed.mutate-in-limit — note both are pressures to *start*: the pressure to begin new experience, and the pressure to mutate. Neither wired. `[firm]`
- **island:** B (body)
- **wires from here:** wire.transformation
- **connects to:** wire.transformation, sealed.completion, sealed.begin-experience, sealed.mutate-in-limit, mechanism.pressure-differential
- **source:** HD · the book: fixed nature; four unwired turn

### Seven fixed — what the count means
- **id:** seven-fixed
- **what it is:** seven of nine fixed. Most people run three to five. Roughly the top few percent. `[read]`
- **not what it means:** not "most people don't know who they are." It's that in most people, several functions are borrowed from whoever's in the room. Hers are the same everywhere.
- **what it means:** unusually constant. Largely unaffected by other people. And correspondingly, other people feel *her* more than she feels them — she transmits a lot and absorbs little. Can be a strong presence without noticing; can miss cues that absorbers would catch.
- **the firehose:** seven fixed functions with a fixed pressure center, pointed at someone with mostly open functions, is a lot. She experiences it as normal presence; they experience it as pressure she cannot perceive, because she has no open functions to feel it through.

## 1.3 The six wires

### The circling mind — mental pressure ↔ processing
- **id:** wire.circling-mind
- **regions:** region.head ↔ region.mind
- **circuitry:** individual (knowing)
- **what it does:** returns to a question over and over until it cracks open. Not linear. Recursive. From outside it looks like rumination. From inside it feels like being stuck on something she can't drop.
- **what it produces:** original insight — not fast, not on demand, but genuinely new. The kind of understanding that arrives whole after months of nothing.
- **its rule:** cannot be scheduled. Cannot force the opening. Her only job is to let it circle without forcing a conclusion and without deciding she's stuck.
- **the structural consequence for finishing:** a mind that circles has no natural moment of "enough." It can always go back. The internal feeling of *this is complete* does not arrive — not because she's indecisive, because the machine doesn't produce that signal. `[firm]` — see `mechanism.supersession`
- **carries:** the unconscious-sun capacity of the life theme (pressure toward the unknowable)
- **connects to:** split, mechanism.supersession, mechanism.finishing-broken-down, life-theme, individual-circuitry
- **source:** HD · the book: channels

### The initiate — identity ↔ will
- **id:** wire.initiate
- **regions:** region.identity ↔ region.will
- **circuitry:** individual (centering)
- **what it does:** shock meets the spirit of self. Takes her into things that break her open, and she comes back changed rather than damaged. The shaman channel — the person who goes first into what others won't.
- **gives:** courage that isn't bravado — does frightening things without especially noticing they were frightening. A capacity to be transformed — metabolizes shock that would permanently damage someone else. Competitive drive — a real need to be the one who goes into it.
- **costs:** shocks other people without meaning to — her baseline is further out than theirs. May seek shock when stagnant — without a natural disruption, can generate one: start a fight, blow something up, leave something good. The falls are real and hurt each time.
- **connects to:** region.identity, region.will, individual-circuitry
- **source:** HD · the book: channels

### Following convictions — identity ↔ engine
- **id:** wire.following-convictions
- **regions:** region.identity ↔ region.engine
- **circuitry:** integration
- **what it does:** puts the engine behind being who she is.
- **practically:** when doing something genuinely hers — not adapted, not compromised, not shaped to fit — enormous power is available. When doing something that isn't hers, the power isn't there. Compromise costs her more than it costs others because it doesn't just feel bad — it disconnects the engine.
- **connects to:** integration-circuit
- **source:** HD · the book: channels

### Perfected form — identity ↔ instinct
- **id:** wire.perfected-form
- **regions:** region.identity ↔ region.instinct
- **circuitry:** integration
- **what it does:** instinct about what's healthy *for her specifically*. Not general health knowledge. Her body knows what her body needs — food, environment, people, pace — immediately, without reference to what's good for anyone else.
- **the rule:** this instinct is about her and only her. It doesn't transfer. Which means her read on her own direction is excellent, and her read on what someone else should do is a much weaker instrument. `[firm]` — relevant to `mechanism.right-answer-frame`
- **connects to:** integration-circuit, mechanism.right-answer-frame
- **source:** HD · the book: channels; influence turn

### Power — engine ↔ instinct
- **id:** wire.power
- **regions:** region.engine ↔ region.instinct
- **circuitry:** integration
- **what it does:** instinct straight into action. No thinking step. The body knows and the body moves, in the same instant. In this system, called the most powerful channel in the body.
- **what it feels like:** doing the right thing before understanding why. Reacting correctly with no time to consider. Knowing exactly how to handle a situation the moment she's in it.
- **its condition:** only works in the present. Cannot be applied to hypotheticals, planning, or the future. Ask it about next year and it has nothing. Put it in the room and it's already moving.
- **connects to:** integration-circuit
- **source:** HD · the book: channels

### Transformation — instinct ↔ pressure
- **id:** wire.transformation
- **regions:** region.instinct ↔ region.pressure
- **circuitry:** tribal — **her single tribal wire; her one structural connection to other people**
- **what it does:** drives her upward, and filters what's worth going up for. Ambition paired with a survival instinct that reads durability.
- **gives:** real ambition — a drive from the pressure center to become more than she currently is; doesn't switch off when comfortable. A durability filter — looks at something and knows it won't last when everyone's excited; backs unremarkable things because she can feel they'll hold; one of her sharpest instincts, operating below reasoning. Endurance — built for the long climb.
- **costs:** fear of failure lives here — the fear that the thing won't last, that she'll be exposed, that it'll collapse; a permanent resident that doesn't leave on success. Works through recognition, not self-promotion — rises by being seen and invited. May dismiss things too fast — the durability read is quick, confident, and sometimes wrong about things that need time.
- **the instruction it shares with the outlet:** pushing costs everything and returns nothing. Being recognized opens doors. `[firm]`
- **carries:** the conscious-earth capacity of the life theme (durability instinct)
- **connects to:** region.instinct, region.pressure, region.throat, life-theme, convergence.shaping-from-outside
- **source:** HD · the book: channels

### The integration circuit — the closed triangle
- **id:** integration-circuit
- **wires:** wire.following-convictions + wire.perfected-form + wire.power
- **regions:** region.identity + region.engine + region.instinct — three functions wired to each other in a closed loop
- **what it is:** the oldest circuitry in the system; predates cooperation. About one organism surviving and thriving as itself, alone. Not selfishness — self-sufficiency at a cellular level. Having all three wires is uncommon. `[read]`
- **gives:** total self-direction — no need for permission, approval, instruction, or a map; finds her own way to everything. Instinctive competence — good at things without being taught, because learning happens in the doing. Resilience — survival circuitry; will find a way through.
- **costs:** **cannot be told what to do** — not "doesn't like it"; cannot metabolize it. Instruction enters as noise, even good advice, even from people she respects, even when correct. This is why hierarchy, management, and mentorship are difficult in a way that isn't attitude. **Isolating by design** — other circuits transmit to a group or tribe; this one exists for the individual. She will be alone in her process more than most, and it isn't a mistake she's making. **Doesn't naturally communicate** — the circuit wants the "now" capacity in the outlet to come out as speech; she doesn't have it. Enormous instinctive power with no built-in way to explain it. Knows exactly what to do and can't say why.
- **the structural fact about input:** it's a closed loop. Nothing enters from outside. That's why instruction reads as noise — the circuit has no input port.
- **the exit it wants:** see `missing-piece.now`
- **connects to:** region.identity, region.engine, region.instinct, missing-piece.now, mechanism.autonomy-resolved, convergence.cannot-be-governed
- **source:** HD · the book: channels; three wires turn

## 1.4 The sealed capacities — present as pressure, absent as mechanism

> A sealed capacity is a door with no matching door elsewhere. The capacity is real. It has no route out on its own.

### Precise naming — in the outlet
- **id:** sealed.precise-naming
- **region:** region.throat
